OBSERVABILITY AND INCIDENT RESPONSE GUIDE (v1.1, DRAFT)

## Tooling
Metrics are collected with Prometheus and visualised in Grafana. Logs are shipped to a central log store.

## Alert Thresholds
- API p95 latency above 500 ms for 5 minutes: warning to the engineering channel.
- Error rate above 2 percent for 3 minutes: page the on-call engineer.
- Database CPU above 80 percent for 10 minutes: warning.

## Severity Levels
- SEV1: customer-facing outage.
- SEV2: major degradation.
- SEV3: minor issue with a workaround.

## Postmortems
A written postmortem is required within 5 business days for any SEV1 or SEV2 incident.
